About Black Buddha FM

A Living Sonic Archive of Black British Bass Culture

Black Buddha FM is an interactive audio documentary and cultural archive celebrating the pioneers, sound systems, DJs, and MCs who built the sonic foundation of modern Britain.

From the arrival of Jamaican sound systems in 1970s community halls to the clandestine rooftop pirate radio transmitters of 1990s London, Black Britain created an unbroken continuum of sonic rebellion that reshaped global music.

Across ten curated chapters, this project traces how heavy dubplate culture and sound system preamps evolved into Jungle, Drum & Bass, UK Garage, Grime, Dubstep, UK Funky, and UK Drill.

"Pirate radio wasn't just music on the airwaves — it was community, resistance, and the sonic blueprint of British youth culture."

— Black Buddha FM Archive Manifesto

We preserve the raw tape rips, handwritten cassette inlay tracklists, tower block broadcast coordinates, and studio hardware that turned local council estate frequencies into worldwide cultural movements.
