Before Jungle had a name, breakbeat hardcore was mutating in London bedrooms and pirate radio studios. This episode traces the exact moment reggae sound-system culture collided with sped-up breaks, sampler trickery and dub-heavy bass to invent something entirely new. From Lennie De Ice's ground-zero blueprint to Deep Blue's futuristic sound design, these ten tracks capture Jungle finding its own identity between 1991 and 1993 — raw, inventive, and built to move a sound system.
